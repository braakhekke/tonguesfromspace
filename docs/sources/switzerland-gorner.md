## Wider source search, round 1 and round 2 (2026-11-03): WGMS records, velocity products, historical maps; panel text audit
**Round 1 (search):** the search for papers, theses and field data of this glacier was done when the ledger above was written; this section adds what was missing for the ten-glacier standard (WGMS, velocity, historical maps) and records the panel text audit.

**WGMS Fluctuations of Glaciers database**, version 2026-02-10 (doi:10.5904/wgms-fog-2026-02-10; the zip of wgms.ch, CSV tables glacier, front_variation, mass_balance, mass_balance_band, mass_balance_point, state and change **read**; values are quoted as the files state them, their units were not re-checked against the FoG readme). WGMS id 391 (Gorner): front_variation 129 rows 1882-07-02 to 2023-08-23; **no mass_balance, band or point records**; state 22 rows 1963 to 2009; change 13 records, among them Hugonnet et al. 1999-12-31 to 2019-12-31 (elevation change -19.96 m as the file states it; 5-year rows -5.45, -5.07, -4.82, -4.605 m for 1999-2004, 2004-2009, 2009-2014, 2014-2019) and older geodetic records 1931-08-15 to 1982-10-15 (-18.6 m) and 1982-10-15 to 2003-08-15 (-15.06 m). Relevance: no mass balance exists for this glacier in WGMS, so nothing to check the donor-band balance against; the geodetic records are decadal, before the Sentinel-2 years.

**Other velocity products (round 2):** Rabatel, Ducasse, Millan, Mouginot, "Annual glacier surface flow velocity product from Sentinel-2 data for the European Alps", V1, 2023, doi:10.57745/XHQ7TL (annual maps 2015 to 2021 at 50 m; dataset page **read**, its licence **NOT READ**, to be checked before any use). Pointers, **NOT READ:** Dehecq et al. (2019) Alps Landsat 8 velocities; Millan et al. (2019); Friedl et al. Sentinel-1 velocities. 

**Ice speed already on the page:** ITS_LIVE (data/itslive, rebuilt 2026-11-03: identical). The flowline has 206 points of which 166 have values; card values 138, 160, 176, 164, 186, 164, 149, 157, 165, 175, 153 and 138 m/yr for 2013 to 2024 (computed, as the card computes it): works.

**Historical maps and outlines (round 2):** SGI1931 (Mannerfelt and Hodel, ETH Zürich 2022, CC BY 4.0 per its GLAMOS page; shapefile and ReadMe **read**; every glacier carries its own `date`); Freudiger, Mennekes, Seibert, Weiler (2018), Earth System Science Data 10, 805-814, doi:10.5194/essd-10-805-2018 (Siegfried sheets): article page **read**, dataset licence **NOT READ**, outlines **not** opened; swisstopo Dufour and Siegfried maps on map.geo.admin.ch: pointer, **NOT READ**. 

**This glacier's outlines (computed from the shapefiles, planar area in the file's own CRS; SGI2016 attribute as stated):** SGI1931 `date` 1930-08-11, 62.44 km2 (computed, LV03); SGI2010 40.24 km2 (+ a 1.35 km2 second polygon); SGI2016 41.24 km2 (year_acq 2015).
