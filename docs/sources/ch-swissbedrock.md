## Result (owner's decision 2026-10-30)

- **The terms of use of the dataset are swisstopo's OGD terms** (the product page links them): use, distribute, process, commercial use allowed, **credit mandatory**: "Federal Office of Topography swisstopo" or "©swisstopo". swissBEDROCK is not among the exceptions with their own wording (swissALTI, Regio, swissEO). The owner decided that this is the licence to follow, also for the glacier parts that come from GLAMOS (2020), because swisstopo publishes the whole dataset under it. Does not make the site commercial.
- Credit line to use: "swissBEDROCK, Federal Office of Topography swisstopo". Under glaciers it is worth naming the model in the text, because it is true and the release notes name it: swissALTI3D 10 m minus modelled ice thickness (GLAMOS 2020), a "maximum estimate" without unconsolidated deposits. Not a legal requirement.
- Not independent of the radar points: the release notes do not say how the GLAMOS model was made (NOT READ), so show the model bed as a model, next to the measured radar, not as a check on it.
- The CC BY 4.0 of the GLAMOS DOI page is for the radar points (a separate dataset, already on the site).
- Still NOT READ: the geocat record's constraints (does not change the above).
