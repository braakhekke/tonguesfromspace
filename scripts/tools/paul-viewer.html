Paul ratio viewer

Sentinel-2 L2A, one scene per year, drawn in a canvas from the raw bands. The ice map follows the clean-ice rule of Paul et al. (2020): (red / SWIR) > th1 and blue > th2.

From the paper: bands 2, 3, 4, 8 and 11, band 11 (20 m) bilinearly resampled to 10 m; clean ice by "(red/SWIR)>th1 and blue>th2", thresholds set per scene; in the Leschaux test site th1 = 3.0 and th2 = 860; no smoothing filter. The paper does not define a false-colour composite; "SWIR / NIR / red" is the common glacier composite and is our choice, not the paper's. Snow patches under 0.01 km², shadow and debris handling of the paper are not reproduced here. Reflectance here is the L2A value × 10000 as returned by Sentinel Hub, which may not be the same scale as the paper's 2015 scenes, so th2 is a starting point. Paul et al. 2020, ESSD 12, 1805: essd.copernicus.org/articles/12/1805/2020.
