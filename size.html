Tongues from Space Glacier sizes

How big is a glacier? All ten glaciers lie where they really are. Pick one, drag it onto a city or another glacier, and turn it with its round handle.

01Glacier

    Click a glacier here or on the map. The last two you pick are compared on the right. The outlines are the latest available ones, from the Swiss Glacier Inventory SGI2023 (state of 2023; Unteraar and Trift 2024; GLAMOS). The glaciers have shrunk since.

    02Compare with a city

    City outlines are the municipal boundaries (swissBOUNDARIES3D), so they include lake area where the boundary does.

    Format
