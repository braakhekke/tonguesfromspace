Ice edge lines (experimental)

The layer "Ice edge (ablation zone)" draws the margin of the bare ice at the lower end of the glacier, computed here from the Level-2A scene of that year. Snow and ice are picked with the snow index NDSI = (B03 − B11) / (B03 + B11) above 0.4 and near infrared (B08) above 0.11, the usual snow thresholds; the darker of those pixels (red band B04 up to 0.45, our own threshold, set by eye and not validated) count as bare ice. Gaps up to 60 m, such as moraine stripes, are closed. Lines follow the border between bare ice and bare ground inside the 1850 outline and stay open at the snow line, under clouds and where data are missing.

Debris-covered ice looks like rock and is not found, so on such tongues the line ends above the real tip (about 1 km on Aletsch). On a 2023 scene the method found 82 to 95 % of the SGI2023 area as snow or ice for eight glaciers, but only about 54 % for Unteraar and 53 % for Zmutt. The lines are no official outlines.
