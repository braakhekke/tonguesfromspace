Pick scenes

One frame per year for a glacier timelapse. Every Sentinel-2 acquisition between 1 August and the end date is scored over the tongue window (the area within a radius of the tongue tip, cut to the image area of the site): cloud, cloud shadow, thin cirrus, snow, and haze (aerosol optical thickness), with a preference for scenes near 30 September, the end of the glaciological year. A hard rule keeps out every scene with cloud, cloud shadow or a big mountain shadow in the bottom 3 km of the tongue, and every scene with much cloud anywhere in the image (the limits are set next to the Search button). An orbit with a year that has no scene is marked incomplete and cannot be the lowest-cost orbit. Pictures are also compared with the other pictures of the same orbit, which catches what the numbers miss (haze, shadows, snow patches on the rock around the glacier). For every orbit the tool finds the date of the year that gives the tightest, cleanest timelapse; you choose the orbit and swap single years. The result is saved as scenes.json.
