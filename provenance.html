Kinds of surface

measured: a dated DEM or lidar surface. interpolated: between two measured surfaces. extrapolated: beyond the last one, at its rate. provisional: 2026. Date: every year is the surface on 30 September of that year (the end of the glaciological year). A measured surface is moved from its survey day to 30 September by the same model, so the surface shown for a surveyed year is not the raw survey: it is the survey surface lowered by the melt between the survey day and 30 September (for a survey in August, the weeks in between).

Sharing the loss between years

Solid: the GLAMOS balance sets how the loss between two measured surfaces falls into each year. Dashed: extrapolated at the last segment's rate (2026 also with its own balance). Not used: measured years.

Uncertainty

Median over the tongue ice, red whisker the 90th percentile. Calibrated on the same ICESat-2 shots. 2026 has none yet: it is provisional and its balance was read from the GLAMOS figure.

Independent checks

ICESat-2 also fitted the 2023 anchor day and σ, so it is not independent (rmse is leave-one-granule-out). ASTER and stakes are checks only. Click a year for their numbers.
